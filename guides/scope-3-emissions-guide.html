Scope 3 Emissions — The 15 Categories Explained

Scope 3 is usually 70–95% of a company’s footprint and the hardest part of any inventory. This guide maps the 15 GHG Protocol categories, shows where to start by sector, and explains how supplier engagement upgrades data quality over time.

Key facts — verified & sourced

The 15 Scope 3 categories

  • Upstream: 1 purchased goods & services, 2 capital goods, 3 fuel & energy related, 4 upstream transport, 5 waste, 6 business travel, 7 commuting, 8 upstream leased assets.
  • Downstream: 9 downstream transport, 10 processing of sold products, 11 use of sold products, 12 end-of-life, 13 downstream leased assets, 14 franchises, 15 investments (financed emissions).
  • For most financial institutions, category 15 dominates — often >95% of total footprint.

Source: ghgprotocol.org · reviewed 2026-07

Supplier engagement (CDP Supply Chain pattern)

  • Effective supplier programmes tier by spend and emissions: top ~20 suppliers usually cover 60–80% of category-1 emissions — start disclosure requests there.
  • Escalation ladder: information request → improvement plan → contractual clauses → procurement preference/exclusion; CDP Supply Chain membership operationalises the request step.
  • Ask for supplier-specific (not industry-average) emission factors as maturity grows — it converts spend-based estimates into activity-based data.

Source: cdp.net · reviewed 2026-07

SBTi reduction pathways

  • SBTi's cross-sector 1.5°C pathway requires a minimum 4.2% absolute reduction per year for Scope 1+2 near-term targets.
  • Scope 3 targets are required when Scope 3 is ≥40% of total emissions; near-term Scope 3 ambition minimum is well-below-2°C (≈2.5%/yr).
  • The Net-Zero Standard requires 90–95% absolute reduction by 2050 with residual emissions neutralised by permanent removals.

Source: sciencebasedtargets.org · reviewed 2026-07

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to measure all 15 categories?

No — you must assess all 15 for relevance and justify exclusions. Most companies find 3–6 categories material.

Spend-based or activity-based?

Start spend-based (EEIO factors × procurement spend) for coverage, then upgrade the biggest categories to activity- or supplier-specific data.

When does SBTi require a Scope 3 target?

When Scope 3 is at least 40% of your total footprint — which is true for most non-extractive sectors.
