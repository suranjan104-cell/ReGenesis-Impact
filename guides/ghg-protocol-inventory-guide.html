GHG Protocol Inventory — How to Measure Scope 1, 2 and 3

Every climate disclosure regime builds on a GHG Protocol inventory. This guide covers the decisions that determine whether yours survives assurance: boundary choice, factor vintages, dual Scope 2 reporting and the audit trail.

Key facts — verified & sourced

GHG Protocol scopes and boundaries

  • Scope 1 = direct emissions from owned/controlled sources; Scope 2 = purchased energy; Scope 3 = all other value-chain emissions across 15 defined categories.
  • Organisational boundary uses either equity share or control (financial or operational) — the choice must be documented and applied consistently.
  • Scope 2 dual reporting: both location-based (grid average) and market-based (contractual instruments) figures are required by the Scope 2 Guidance.

Source: ghgprotocol.org · reviewed 2026-07

Common emission factors (DEFRA 2024)

  • Diesel ≈ 2.68 kgCO2e/litre; petrol ≈ 2.31 kgCO2e/litre; natural gas ≈ 56.1 kgCO2e/GJ (≈2.04 kg/m³).
  • Average economy long-haul flight ≈ 0.15–0.20 kgCO2e per passenger-km including radiative forcing; UK DEFRA publishes updated factors every June.
  • Landfilled mixed municipal waste ≈ 0.45–0.6 tCO2e per tonne depending on composition.

Source: gov.uk · reviewed 2026-07

Verification and audit-trail expectations

  • Verifiers (ISAE 3410 / ISSA 5000) trace each inventory line to: activity data source, emission factor + vintage, calculation, and responsible owner.
  • Classic findings: stale emission factors (3+ years old), unit errors (GJ vs MWh), market-based claims without retired certificates, and double counting across scopes.
  • Keep a base-year recalculation policy — structural changes (M&A, divestment, methodology) above a threshold (commonly 5%) trigger restatement.

Source: iaasb.org · reviewed 2026-07

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between location- and market-based Scope 2?

Location-based uses the grid-average factor where you consume electricity; market-based reflects your contracts (RECs, PPAs, green tariffs). The Scope 2 Guidance requires reporting both.

Which emission factors should I use?

The most recent official set for your geography — e.g. India CEA for grid electricity, UK DEFRA for fuels and travel — and record the vintage of every factor.

What do verifiers check first?

Factor vintages, unit conversions (GJ vs MWh), completeness of the entity list, and certificates behind any market-based claims.
