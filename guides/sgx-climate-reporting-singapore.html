Singapore Climate Reporting — SGX and MAS Requirements

Singapore moves listed issuers to mandatory ISSB-aligned climate reporting from FY2025, with large non-listed companies following from FY2027, while MAS expects financial institutions to manage environmental risk end-to-end. Here is the combined picture.

Key facts — verified & sourced

Singapore SGX climate reporting timeline

  • SGX-listed issuers move to mandatory ISSB-aligned climate reporting from FY2025, starting with larger issuers; large non-listed companies (revenue ≥ S$1bn) follow from FY2027.
  • Reporting was previously phased comply-or-explain from FY2022 under SGX's sustainability reporting regime, with mandatory climate reporting for finance, agri-food and energy sectors from FY2023.

Source: acra.gov.sg · reviewed 2026-07

MAS environmental risk management (Singapore)

  • MAS ERM Guidelines (2020) require banks, insurers and asset managers to embed environmental risk in governance, risk management and disclosure, with board-level accountability.
  • MAS industry stress tests have included long-run climate scenarios aligned to NGFS pathways; disclosure expectations reference TCFD/ISSB alignment.

Source: mas.gov.sg · reviewed 2026-07

Frequently asked questions

When does mandatory reporting start?

SGX-listed issuers report ISSB-aligned climate disclosures from FY2025; large non-listed companies (revenue ≥ S$1bn) from FY2027.

What do the MAS ERM guidelines require?

Board-level accountability for environmental risk, integration into risk frameworks, scenario analysis and disclosure — for banks, insurers and asset managers.

Is assurance required in Singapore?

Not yet mandated, but the regulatory roadmap strongly signals it — preparing evidence as if assurance applies is the low-regret move.
