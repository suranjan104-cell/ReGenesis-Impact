PCAF Financed Emissions — A Guide for Banks and Investors

For financial institutions, category 15 (investments) dwarfs everything else. PCAF is the global standard for measuring it. This guide covers the attribution formula, the seven asset classes and the data-quality scoring that regulators increasingly expect.

Key facts — verified & sourced

PCAF financed emissions methodology

  • PCAF attribution: financed emissions = (outstanding amount ÷ EVIC or total equity+debt) × borrower/investee emissions.
  • Data quality scores run 1 (audited, reported emissions) to 5 (economic-activity proxies); portfolios disclose an exposure-weighted average score.
  • PCAF Standard v2 (2022) covers seven asset classes including listed equity, business loans, project finance, mortgages, commercial real estate and sovereign debt.

Source: carbonaccountingfinancials.com · reviewed 2026-07

The 15 Scope 3 categories

  • Upstream: 1 purchased goods & services, 2 capital goods, 3 fuel & energy related, 4 upstream transport, 5 waste, 6 business travel, 7 commuting, 8 upstream leased assets.
  • Downstream: 9 downstream transport, 10 processing of sold products, 11 use of sold products, 12 end-of-life, 13 downstream leased assets, 14 franchises, 15 investments (financed emissions).
  • For most financial institutions, category 15 dominates — often >95% of total footprint.

Source: ghgprotocol.org · reviewed 2026-07

GFANZ transition plan framework

  • GFANZ recommends transition plans across five themes: foundations, implementation strategy, engagement strategy, metrics & targets, and governance.
  • Credible plans distinguish financing emissions reduction ('financed reductions') from portfolio decarbonisation via divestment, which shifts rather than cuts real-economy emissions.

Source: gfanzero.com · reviewed 2026-07

Frequently asked questions

What is the PCAF attribution formula?

Financed emissions = (your outstanding amount ÷ the company’s EVIC) × the company’s emissions — summed across the portfolio, by asset class.

What are PCAF data quality scores?

A 1–5 scale per exposure (1 = audited reported emissions, 5 = economic-activity proxies); portfolios disclose the exposure-weighted average and improve it over time.

Does PCAF cover sovereigns and mortgages?

Yes — PCAF v2 covers listed equity/bonds, business loans, project finance, commercial real estate, mortgages, motor vehicle loans and sovereign debt.
