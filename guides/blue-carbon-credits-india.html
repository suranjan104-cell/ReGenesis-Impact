Blue Carbon Credits in India — Mangroves, Seagrass and Beyond

Mangroves and seagrass meadows store carbon in sediment for centuries and shield coastlines while doing it — which is why blue carbon trades at a premium. India’s coastline holds some of the world’s largest restoration potential.

Key facts — verified & sourced

Blue carbon science

  • Mangroves, seagrasses and tidal marshes sequester carbon per hectare at multiples of tropical forests, largely in sediment that stays stored for centuries if undisturbed.
  • Mangroves also deliver storm-surge protection and fisheries co-benefits, supporting premium pricing; Verra VM0033 is the main tidal-wetland methodology.
  • India's coastline (Sundarbans, Tamil Nadu, Odisha, Andamans) hosts significant blue-carbon restoration potential.

Source: thebluecarboninitiative.org · reviewed 2026-07

Registries and standards

  • Verra's VCS is the largest voluntary registry by issuance; Gold Standard emphasises SDG co-benefits; others include ACR, CAR and Puro.earth (removals).
  • Every credit carries: registry ID, methodology, vintage year, project location, and verification body — retirement is recorded publicly on the registry.
  • Article 6.4 of the Paris Agreement is creating a UN-supervised international crediting mechanism alongside voluntary markets.

Source: verra.org · reviewed 2026-07

India's carbon market (CCTS)

  • India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (2023) transitions the PAT energy-efficiency scheme into a compliance carbon market with sectoral intensity targets, alongside a voluntary offset mechanism.
  • Obligated entities span energy-intensive sectors (steel, cement, aluminium, fertiliser); the Bureau of Energy Efficiency administers with CERC as market regulator.

Source: beeindia.gov.in · reviewed 2026-07

Frequently asked questions

Why do blue carbon credits cost more?

Higher per-hectare sequestration, long-lived sediment storage, and stacked co-benefits (storm protection, fisheries, biodiversity) that corporate buyers value.

Which methodology covers tidal wetlands?

Verra VM0033 is the principal tidal wetland and seagrass restoration methodology; Gold Standard also certifies blue carbon projects.

Where is India’s blue carbon potential?

The Sundarbans, Tamil Nadu and Odisha coastlines, and the Andaman Islands — mangrove restoration belts and seagrass meadows.
