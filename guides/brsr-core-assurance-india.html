BRSR & BRSR Core — India’s ESG Reporting and Assurance Rules

SEBI’s Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report applies to India’s top 1,000 listed companies, and BRSR Core adds assured KPIs phasing from the top 150 toward the top 1,000. This guide summarises scope, the KPI set and the assurance requirement.

Key facts — verified & sourced

SEBI BRSR and BRSR Core in India

  • BRSR is mandatory for the top 1,000 listed companies by market cap; BRSR Core adds a subset of KPIs requiring assurance, phased from the top 150 companies (FY2023-24) toward the top 1,000.
  • BRSR Core KPIs cover GHG intensity, water and energy footprint, waste, employee wellbeing/safety spend, gender diversity in wages, and supply-chain metrics.
  • SEBI's 2023 circular also introduced value-chain ESG disclosures for the top 250 companies on a comply-or-explain basis.

Source: sebi.gov.in · reviewed 2026-07

India BRSR Core assurance specifics

  • BRSR Core requires reasonable assurance of specified KPIs, phased from the top 150 listed entities (FY2023-24) expanding toward the top 1,000 by FY2026-27.
  • SEBI later allowed 'assessment or assurance' language and profession-agnostic providers, but the KPI set (GHG intensity, water, energy, safety, diversity, grievances) is fixed.
  • The assurer must be independent — no non-audit conflicts with the assured entity.

Source: sebi.gov.in · reviewed 2026-07

Market-specific DD weightings

  • India-focused ESG assessments commonly weight Social higher (labour standards, community consent, informal-workforce exposure) reflecting BRSR's nine NGRBC principles.
  • Key India red flags: contract-labour compliance, land acquisition/consent history, water stress in operations, and related-party governance.

Source: sebi.gov.in · reviewed 2026-07

Frequently asked questions

Who must file BRSR?

The top 1,000 listed entities by market capitalisation file BRSR with their annual report; smaller listed companies may adopt voluntarily.

What does BRSR Core add?

A defined KPI subset — GHG intensity, water and energy footprint, waste, safety spend, gender pay metrics and more — that requires independent assurance on a phased schedule.

Does BRSR cover the value chain?

Yes — SEBI introduced value-chain ESG disclosures for the top 250 companies on a comply-or-explain basis.
