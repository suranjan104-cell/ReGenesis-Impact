IFRS S2 Climate Disclosure — Practical Guide

IFRS S2 is the ISSB’s climate disclosure standard, now adopted or being adopted in 30+ jurisdictions including Australia and Singapore, and interoperable with the ESRS on climate. This guide covers what the standard actually asks for and how to produce a defensible first report.

Key facts — verified & sourced

IFRS S2 core structure

  • IFRS S2 requires disclosure across four TCFD-aligned pillars: Governance (paras 5–9), Strategy (paras 8–23), Risk Management (paras 24–26), Metrics & Targets (paras 27–37).
  • Scope 1, 2 and 3 GHG emissions are mandatory under para 29(a), measured per the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard unless a jurisdiction requires otherwise.
  • Scope 2 must be reported location-based, with market-based information disclosed where contractual instruments exist.

Source: ifrs.org · reviewed 2026-07

IFRS S2 transition reliefs

  • First-year reliefs: entities may omit Scope 3 emissions, comparatives, and may report sustainability disclosures with second-quarter interim reporting rather than with the annual report.
  • The Scope 3 relief lasts one annual reporting period; after that, Scope 3 measurement per the GHG Protocol Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard is required.

Source: ifrs.org · reviewed 2026-07

Materiality assessment good practice

  • A defensible materiality process documents: stakeholder groups consulted, scoring criteria, threshold for 'material', board sign-off, and refresh cadence (typically annual or biennial).
  • Under IFRS S1/S2, information is material if omitting it could influence investor decisions — impact-only topics belong in GRI/CSRD-style reports instead.

Source: ifrs.org · reviewed 2026-07

Frequently asked questions

Is Scope 3 mandatory under IFRS S2?

Yes — paragraph 29(a) requires Scope 1, 2 and 3 measured per the GHG Protocol, but a one-year transition relief lets first-time reporters omit Scope 3 in year one.

How does IFRS S2 relate to TCFD?

IFRS S2 fully incorporates the TCFD architecture (Governance, Strategy, Risk Management, Metrics & Targets), so an existing TCFD report is the natural starting point.

Do small companies need IFRS S2?

Only where a jurisdiction mandates it — thresholds vary by market (see the Australia and Singapore guides). Voluntary adoption still builds investor credibility.
