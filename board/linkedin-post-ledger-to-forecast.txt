From ledger to forecast: what climate scenario analysis requires that reporting never collected.

Reporting produces a number for last year. IFRS S2 and AASB S2 also require a resilience assessment built on forward-looking climate scenarios, and a transition plan disclosed against it. That is a different data problem, and largely one nobody has been collecting for.

Three findings from the briefing:

— Around 70% of reported emissions in the ECB's stress test rested on proxies rather than counterparty data.
— The paper behind the physical-risk estimates in NGFS Phase V has been retracted. Resilience assessments built on those outputs carry an assumption worth disclosing.
— Scenario analysis and transition plans sit inside the three-year modified liability window and attract no assurance in the first Australian reporting year.
