A bank's financed emissions can fall by a third without a single borrower cutting a tonne.

Attribution divides exposure by counterparty value. For listed equity that value is EVIC — a market price. Valuations rise, the denominator rises, the reported figure falls. Nothing changed in the real economy.

A new briefing on Scope 3 Category 15: how the calculation works, the five places it breaks, and the route out that does not exist — no emission factor database lifts a portfolio above PCAF data quality score 3, because scores 1 and 2 only exist where the counterparty supplied the data.

Useful for teams building a financed emissions inventory ahead of assurance, and for anyone deciding whether to buy data or go and collect it.
