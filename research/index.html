We audited our own emission factors

Our assurance tool told users to build a factor register and we did not have one. So we built it, ran our own code against it, and published what it found — including one factor living in three places and a project finance intensity 4,000× too low.

On sourcing. These briefings were written under a hard constraint: any figure that could not be traced to a primary source was excluded, not hedged. That is also why one previously published estimate was withdrawn mid-draft — the paper behind it had been retracted. The same discipline governs our emission factor register, which publishes the provenance, vintage and confidence of every factor our calculators apply, including the ones we cannot yet source.
