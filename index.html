Frameworks · Methodology · Compliance guides

IFRS S2 / ISSB S2 Compliance Guide

IFRS S2 (effective January 2024) requires all mandatory reporters to disclose climate-related risks under four pillars: Governance, Strategy, Risk Management and Metrics & Targets. The December 2025 amendments introduced enhanced Scope 3 transition plan disclosures. Our AI-powered generator covers all 10 required disclosure areas and produces board-ready output aligned to TCFD and NGFS v5 scenarios.

SEBI BRSR Mandatory Reporting (India)

SEBI's Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR) is mandatory for India's top 1,000 listed companies by market cap from FY 2022–23. BRSR Core KPI assured disclosures are required for the top 150 from FY 2023–24. Our BRSR module covers Section A (General Disclosures), Section B (Management) and Section C (9 Principles) including ESG attributes for value chain partners.

GHG Protocol Scope 3 & PCAF Financed Emissions

Scope 3 Category 15 financed emissions represent over 95% of a bank's or asset manager's total carbon footprint. The PCAF (Partnership for Carbon Accounting Financials) standard provides methodology for loans, bonds, listed equity, project finance and mortgages. Our calculator covers all 15 Scope 3 categories with IPCC AR6 GWP factors, PCAF data quality scores and WACI intensity metrics.

TCFD Scenario Analysis with NGFS v5

TCFD-aligned scenario analysis is now mandatory under IFRS S2 and RBI climate risk guidelines for Indian banks. NGFS Phase V (2024) provides four pathways: Net Zero 2050, Orderly 2°C, Disorderly Transition and Hot House World. Our platform quantifies transition risk (carbon price exposure, policy risk) and physical risk (flood, heat, drought CVaR) for each scenario across your asset portfolio.

SBTi Net Zero Targets & Decarbonisation Roadmap

The Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) requires companies to cut Scope 1+2 emissions 50% by 2030 and Scope 3 by 90% before 2050 to stay within 1.5°C. Our MACC (Marginal Abatement Cost Curve) tool identifies the lowest-cost decarbonisation levers for your sector — solar, efficiency, fleet electrification, supply chain engagement — with capex, payback and abatement potential for each lever.

GRI Standards, ASSB & CDP Disclosure

Our Sustainability Disclosure Hub supports five global frameworks in one platform. GRI Standards (GRI 2, GRI 305, GRI 302) for universal sustainability reporting. ASSB/AASB S2 for Australian mandatory reporters from 2025. CDP Climate Change questionnaire (C1–C7) for investor and customer disclosure. All frameworks share your company profile data — switch frameworks without re-entering information.

AASB S2 Mandatory Climate Reporting — Australia 2025

Australia's AASB S2 (Australian Sustainability Reporting Standard) aligns with ISSB S2 and is mandatory for Group 1 entities (consolidated revenue > AUD $500M or total assets > $1B) from annual periods beginning 1 January 2025. Group 2 follows from 2026 and Group 3 from 2027. APRA simultaneously requires Climate Vulnerability Assessments (CVA) under CPS 229 for ADIs and insurers — covering physical risk to balance sheets and transition risk stress testing. ASIC's RG 247 provides enforcement guidance on misleading climate statements. Our platform generates AASB S2-compliant reports, runs APRA CVA modelling, and aligns with ASIC's disclosure obligations in a single workflow.

MAS Environmental Risk & SGX Climate Reporting — Singapore

The Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) requires all banks, insurers and asset managers under MAS Notice 645 and the Guidelines on Environmental Risk Management (ERM) to integrate climate risk into governance, strategy and risk appetite. SGX-listed companies must report climate-related disclosures aligned with ISSB S2 — mandatory for large-cap from FY2024 and mid-cap from FY2025. Singapore's Green Finance Action Plan (GFAP) 2.0 sets out the roadmap for the Singapore-Asia Taxonomy and alignment with international standards. ReGenesis Impact covers MAS ERM requirements, SGX ISSB S2 disclosures, and the Singapore-Asia Taxonomy classification in one integrated platform.

🔬 ESG CREDIT RISK METHODOLOGY
BASEL III ESG INTEGRATION · RBI SUSTAINABLE FINANCE DIRECTIVE · TCFD SCENARIO
Methodology: This tool integrates ESG risk factors into traditional credit assessment using a modified Altman Z-score approach, adjusted for sector-specific ESG materiality weights per SASB standards.

ESG Adjustment Factor: The composite ESG score (0–100) generates a credit adjustment of ±15% on the base probability of default (PD), consistent with ECB supervisory expectations and RBI's draft Climate Risk guidelines (2024).

Sector Materiality: ESG risk weights are adjusted by sector. High-carbon sectors (O&G, Steel, Power) carry higher E-weight (40%). Financial services carry higher G-weight (45%). Consumer/Healthcare carry higher S-weight (35%).

TCFD Scenarios: Transition risk is assessed under IEA Net Zero 2050 and Delayed Transition scenarios. Physical risk is assessed under RCP 4.5 and RCP 8.5.

Regulatory References: Basel III Pillar 2, EBA Guidelines on ESG risks (2021), RBI Discussion Paper on Climate Risk (2024), SEBI BRSR Core, NGFS Climate Scenarios.

Financed Emissions (PCAF)
Banks and asset managers use PCAF attribution factors to apportion their investee companies' emissions. Formula: Financed Emissions = (Outstanding Loan ÷ Enterprise Value) × Borrower's Total Emissions. Tokenised credits can be allocated back to specific loan portfolios — each retirement is tagged with the investee company ID, automatically reducing the bank's attributed Scope 3 Cat 15 position. Data quality score (1–5) tracked per PCAF Standard v2.

Supply Chain (Cat 1–14)
Upstream and downstream Scope 3 categories are tracked by linking purchase orders → supplier emissions data. Suppliers upload their emissions certificates (tokenised or PDF). The platform uses spend-based and physical unit methods per GHG Protocol Scope 3 Standard. Category 1 (purchased goods), Cat 4 (upstream transport) and Cat 11 (use of sold products) are the highest-materiality categories for most sectors. Each supplier's verified emissions token links directly to your Cat 1 inventory line item.
