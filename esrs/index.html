Questions

Am I in scope for the CSRD after the Omnibus?

An EU company is in scope only if it exceeds both 1,000 employees and €450mn net turnover. The Omnibus made this an AND test, which took a large number of previously in-scope companies out. Non-EU parent groups are tested on EU-generated turnover above €450mn.

Which ESRS apply to a 2026 financial year?

ESRS Set 1, Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2772. The revised simplified standards were adopted on 3 July 2026 and cut mandatory datapoints by 61%, but they apply to financial years beginning on or after 1 January 2027 — so a FY2026 report is prepared under Set 1.

How are the EU Taxonomy Article 8 KPIs calculated?

Three KPIs — turnover, CapEx and OpEx — each an aligned numerator over its own denominator. Alignment needs four gates: eligible, substantial contribution to one of the six environmental objectives, do no significant harm to the other five, and entity-level minimum safeguards. Safeguards are assessed once and applied to everything, so failing them zeroes alignment across all three KPIs.

Do I have to report the OpEx KPI?

Not necessarily. Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/73 allows the OpEx KPI to be omitted where it is not material to the business model, disclosing only its total value and why. The same regulation lets you skip assessing any activity below 10% of a KPI, applied to each KPI independently.

When does XBRL tagging become mandatory?

There is no date. Mandatory tagging of the sustainability statement is suspended by Directive (EU) 2026/470, in force 18 March 2026, until the ESEF Delegated Regulation is amended to cover it — which requires ESMA technical standards that have not been drafted. Any vendor quoting you a resumption date is guessing.

Is my IFRS S2 materiality assessment enough?

No. ESRS reports a topic material to people and the environment or to the company; IFRS S2 covers only the second. The impact side has to be assessed separately, and every omitted topical standard needs a stated reason.

Is it really free, and where does my data go?

Free, no sign-up. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded — which is exactly why it can be used on live commercial figures before a vendor has been cleared.
